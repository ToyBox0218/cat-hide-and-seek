# Cat recording provenance

Cat recordings: **Kerzoven**, [Cat Purr & Meow](https://opengameart.org/content/cat-purr-meow), April 16, 2022.

License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source identifies these as the uploader’s own cat recordings. No endorsement is implied.

Included clips:
- `cat-meow-soft.wav`: `cat_softmew.wav`, original time 1.33–1.92 seconds.
- `cat-meow-food.wav`: `cat_mewfood.wav`, original time 0.54–1.00 seconds.
- `cat-meow-purr.wav`: `cat_mewpurr.wav`, original time 0.00–0.98 seconds.

Processing: trim, DC removal, 6 ms fade-in, 35 ms fade-out, peak normalization to −6 dBFS. No source pitch change. Playback may use a small pitch/rate variation.

Exact original download links, hashes and transformation details are in `provenance.json`. Originals are retained in the development workspace. The source notes movement/background noise; the short cuts were selected from waveform/spectral inspection. They have not been auditioned by ear or in a browser in this environment.
